anh-nha (ảnh nhà)

A custom Android photo sync client for self-hosted Immich servers, accessible via Tailscale VPN.

Features:
- Auto-upload photos to your Immich server over WiFi when Tailscale is connected
- Free local storage after verified sync (safe delete of synced photos)
- Visible offline sync queue with retry
- Multi-device family dashboard showing per-device sync status
- Backup integrity verification (checksum comparison)
- Works without Google Play Services on Android 9+ (API 28)

Privacy:
- No telemetry, no analytics
- No data ever leaves your Tailscale network
- No dependency on Google Play Services

This app is designed for personal/family use with a self-hosted Immich server. It is not affiliated with the Immich project.

Distribution: F-Droid and direct APK.
